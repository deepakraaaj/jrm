Operating guardrails

What makes the design safe to ship

Evidence before language

Every advisory points to normalized observations. The LLM can explain a decision but cannot invent an incident or override the safety engine.

Freshness is a first-class field

Every provider observation carries validity, quality, provenance and expiry. Partial data is visible rather than silently treated as complete.

Degrade deliberately

Provider failures use bounded retry, circuit breakers and stale-data policy. Predefined voice templates keep alerts reliable when AI is unavailable.

Synthetic examples for engineering discussion · Provider-neutral contracts · Safety-critical actions remain deterministic and auditable.
